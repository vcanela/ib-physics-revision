The Trap Field Guide

The twenty ways IB Physics Paper 1A catches people, ranked by how often the 2025 papers used each one. Learn to see the trap and the mark takes care of itself.

Built from the full 2025 assessment year: 299 questions across ten papers (May and November sessions), 966 tagged wrong-answer traps. The frequencies describe one assessment year; they are a guide to where to put your effort, not a prophecy about your paper. Every practice question below is original, written to the same design as a real 2025 question, and pitched at standard level so that every question here is fair game for everyone.

How to use it: pick a trap from the grid, read what it feels like from the inside, then open a question and attempt it before revealing anything. The anatomy tells you which error built each wrong answer. The defence line is what you say to yourself in the exam. The three questions on a trap sit in three different themes on purpose: a trap is a habit, not a topic.
