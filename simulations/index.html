IB Physics Simulations

Small interactive aids for IB Diploma Physics revision. Each one covers a single concept: explore it, quiz yourself on it, and see how the quantities scale. Best used alongside past-paper problems, not instead of them.

Theme A: space, time and motion
Motion Graph TranslatorA1SL One motion, three graphs: match x-t, v-t and a-t stories, then watch the area under v-t become displacement, live.
Theme B: the particulate nature of matter
Blackbody Curves: Stefan-Boltzmann and WienB1SL Slide the temperature: the peak obeys Wien's inverse law while the power grows as T⁴. Scaling quiz and an L ∝ r²T⁴ star builder. Resistor Network TrainerB5SL Series, parallel and combined networks with live currents and p.d.s; ratio quizzes that hunt the inverted-ratio trap.
Theme C: wave behaviour
SHM: the Irrelevant Mass and the Phase StoryC1SL + HL Pendulum and spring side by side: watch which knobs change which period (mass fools the pendulum crowd), then the x, v, a phase view. Transverse Wave: Snapshot vs HistoryC2SL The pattern travels, the particles do not. Predict which way particle P moves next, and stop swapping wavelength for period on graphs. Standing Waves in Strings and PipesC4SL Step through the harmonics of strings and pipes; the closed pipe's even buttons are dead on purpose. Odd harmonics, derived not memorised. Doppler WavefrontsC5SL + HL Watch wavefronts bunch ahead of a moving source, drill the sign discipline, and see why moving source and moving observer are not the same.
Theme D: fields
Force on a Current-Carrying WireD3SL F = BIL sin θ and the right-hand rule: explore directions, quiz yourself, and see how the force scales with angle. Charged Particle in Electric and Magnetic FieldsD3SL F = qv × B and F = qE, with negative charges included; circular paths with r = mv/qB; the velocity selector. Field and Potential of Two SourcesD1 + D2SL + HL Probe the line between two masses or charges: where the field cancels, where the potential does, and why they are never the same place. Lenz's Law and Electromagnetic InductionD4HL Magnet and coil with the four-step direction chain, induced-current quizzes, and why maximum flux can mean zero emf.
Theme E: nuclear and quantum physics
Energy Level TransitionsE1SL + HL Emission and absorption between hydrogen levels: build transitions, count spectral lines, and untangle the E, f, λ relationships. The Photoelectric EffectE2HL Frequency sets the energy, intensity sets the count: cross the threshold, drill the roles, and read the Einstein line whose slope is h. Radioactive Decay: the N-Z BoardE3SL + HL Every decay is a move on the board: walk α and β chains with honest bookkeeping, then ride the half-life curve.
Cross-theme
The Scaling DojoALLSL + HL Pure proportionality drill across the whole syllabus: T⁴, √l, 1/r² and friends. Write the power, apply the factor, defeat the exponent-error.
