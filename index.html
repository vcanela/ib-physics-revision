IB Physics Revision

Revision aids for IB Diploma Physics: worksheets to work through, simulations to build intuition, and guides to the traps the papers set. These are aids, not the revision itself. You revise by solving problems and by drawing your own diagrams; everything here exists to help you see something the page cannot show on its own.

Worksheets
Topic Warm-up Worksheets24 sheets One sheet per syllabus topic, about 30 to 40 minutes of written work each, climbing from definitions to explanation. Worked answers attached, so attempt the sheet first and then check. HL-only topics and parts are flagged.
Interactive simulations
The Simulation Collection15 sims Fifteen single-concept simulations across Themes A to E: explore it, quiz yourself, and watch how the quantities scale. Motion graphs, blackbody curves, SHM, standing waves, Doppler, fields, decay, the photoelectric effect and more.
Guides
Exam Craft Four habits for Paper 1A that are not about knowing more physics: pacing, why letter-hunting is worthless, why no theme is safe to skip, and drawing before you check. The Trap Field Guide The twenty ways Paper 1A catches people, ranked by how often the 2025 papers used each one. Learn to see the trap and the mark takes care of itself. Concept Cards The ideas Paper 1A asked about most in 2025, each paired with the mistake the examiners design for. Front: the physics. Flip: the trap and the defence.
Tools
Pace Traineropens separately Train your sense of timing with the clock hidden. Set the number of questions and the minutes, then tap as you finish each one; afterwards it shows you where your time actually went. Set 40 questions in 60 minutes for HL Paper 1A.
Wider reading
Women Who Changed Physics Eight physicists who altered our understanding of the universe, and the uneven record of who got credit for it. Not revision: read it because the physics is worth knowing and the history is worth arguing about.
